Before you visit

A few things
you might ask.

Have a different question? A call or WhatsApp message is the simplest way to reach us.

Call +91 79979 11390
How do I book an appointment?
Tap any Book Appointment button — WhatsApp opens with a message ready to send, and we’ll confirm your slot. You can also call +91 79979 11390.
When are you open?
We’re open Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 8 PM. On Sundays we see patients by appointment, so message or call us to plan a Sunday visit. Do also check with us about holidays or a particular doctor’s availability.
Where are you located?
Road No. 3, Ayodhya Nagar, Kakinada, Andhra Pradesh 533001. The “Get directions” button links to the current AJ Dental & Facial Aesthetics Google profile.
I’m unsure which treatment I need. What should I choose?
Book a general dental consultation. You can discuss what’s been bothering you at your visit; you don’t need to decide on a procedure before speaking with us.
What should I bring to my first visit?
Bring any relevant previous dental records, X-rays and a list of current medicines, if you have them. If you are unsure what is needed, just ask us when you book. Please do not send medical records through this website.
Can I enquire about facial aesthetics or hair care?
Yes — use the aesthetics or hair-care WhatsApp buttons above, or mention it when you call. Ask which consultations are currently available and which practitioner would be appropriate for your concern.
Was the clinic earlier known as Jubeda Dental?
Yes — we were earlier known as Jubeda Dental, and we’re now AJ Dental & Facial Aesthetics. Please use our current address and phone number on this website when planning your visit.
